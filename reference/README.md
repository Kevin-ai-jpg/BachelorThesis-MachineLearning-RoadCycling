# Reference tables

Small CSVs typed from published papers (task 1.3). Every row carries a `source` column
(short citation + table/figure number) so each number can be checked against the PDF.

Planned files:

- `pro_power_profile_men.csv` — Leo et al. 2022, record power profile of male professionals
- `pro_power_profile_women.csv` — Leo et al. 2022, female professionals
- `level_durability.csv` — Leo et al. 2024, U23 vs ProTeam vs WorldTour, fresh and after kJ/kg
- `u23_vs_pro.csv` — Leo et al. 2021, U23 vs professionals, Tour of the Alps
- `rider_type_profiles.csv` — Pinot & Grappe 2011, profiles by rider type
